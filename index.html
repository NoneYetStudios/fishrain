❓ How to Play Fish Rain

Premise: You are in a small fishing boat trying to catch fish falling from the sky. Unfortunately, fish aren't the only things falling!

  • Steer Your Boat: Use Left/Right Arrow Keys or A/D on a keyboard, hold the On-Screen Steer Buttons on mobile, enable smooth Mouse Follow, or turn on optional Mobile Tilt Steering in Controls.
  • Build Combos: Every consecutive fish or bonus item you catch increases your combo streak up to a 3.0× multiplier (plus +15 end-of-run bonus points per max combo streak).
  • Harmless Junk vs. Deadly Hazards: Catching an Old Boot, Rubber Duck, or Lawn Chair awards 0 points and a funny message—without hurting your hull or resetting your combo. Hitting a Brick, Bowling Ball, Anvil, Fridge, Grand Piano, Fiberglass Cow, or Satellite costs 1 Life and resets your combo!
  • 8 Bodies of Water: Progress from the tiny Puddle through Pond, Lake, Calm River, Swift River, Bay, Coast, and the Open Ocean. Watch out for river currents, squall crosswinds, and towering Rogue Waves!

⚙️ Controls & Tilt Calibration

Choose your preferred steering method. Keyboard (Arrow keys / A & D) and On-Screen Left/Right Buttons always remain available at any time!
